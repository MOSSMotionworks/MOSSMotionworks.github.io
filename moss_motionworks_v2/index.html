03 / The person behind the work

About.

I'm Laurence A. Cezanne Moss, a photographer, cinematographer, and multimedia producer with eleven years of professional experience across commercial, documentary, and public-sector production.

My background includes four years in multimedia communications for the City of Santa Fe and seven years of freelance commercial and industrial photography and videography for galleries, curators, collectors, private collections, and museums.

I work across still photography, cinematography, editing, sound, and visual communications, with a particular interest in stories rooted in people and place.
