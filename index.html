03 / Behind the Work

About.

I'm Laurence A. Cezanne Moss, a photographer, cinematographer and multimedia producer based in Santa Fe, New Mexico, with eleven years of professional experience.

My background includes four years in multimedia communications for the City of Santa Fe and seven years of freelance commercial and industrial photography and videography for galleries, curators, collectors, private collections and museums.

My work spans still photography, documentary filmmaking, cinematography, editing, sound and visual communications, with a particular interest in people, landscapes and the environments that shape their stories.

I founded MOSS Motionworks, LLC in 2026 as an independent home for my photographic and motion-picture work.
